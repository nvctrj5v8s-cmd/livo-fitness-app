Withdraw from contract

Here you can withdraw from your Lookin Premium contract with Mhd Khair Shikho (the provider of Lookin), including without signing in. You do not need to give a reason. The withdrawal period is 14 days from the conclusion of the contract; sending your declaration before it ends is sufficient. You receive an immediate acknowledgement with the date and time.

Privacy: to process withdrawal, we store your name, email, note and timestamp, plus an IP-address hash for 24 hours to prevent abuse. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
