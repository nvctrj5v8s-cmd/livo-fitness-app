Withdrawal information for Lookin Premium

Applies to consumers purchasing a paid Lookin Premium subscription.

Right of withdrawal

You have the right to withdraw from this contract within fourteen days without giving any reason.

The withdrawal period is fourteen days from the date the contract is concluded.

To exercise your right of withdrawal, you must inform us (Mhd Khair Shikho, Am Grübchen 18, 56203 Höhr-Grenzhausen, telephone: +49 15510 338501, email: lookinsupport@gmail.com) of your decision to withdraw by an unequivocal statement, such as a letter sent by post or an email. You may use the attached model withdrawal form, but this is not obligatory.

You may also exercise your right of withdrawal online at https://app.lookin-foodtracker.de/legal/en/widerruf-erklaeren.html without signing in, or in the app under Profile › Withdraw from contract. If you use this online function, we will promptly send an acknowledgement on a durable medium, such as email, containing information about your declaration and the date and time it was received.

To meet the withdrawal deadline, it is sufficient to send your communication concerning exercise of this right before the withdrawal period expires.

Effects of withdrawal

If you withdraw from this contract, we will reimburse all payments received from you, including delivery costs (except additional costs arising from your choice of a delivery method other than our least expensive standard delivery), without undue delay and no later than fourteen days from the day we are informed of your decision to withdraw. We use the same payment method as the original transaction unless expressly agreed otherwise; you will incur no fees for reimbursement.

If you requested that services begin during the withdrawal period, you must pay a reasonable amount proportionate to the services already provided up to the time you inform us of withdrawal, compared with the full scope of services specified in the contract.

End of withdrawal information

Further information

Cancellation is not withdrawal: Cancelling your subscription in the store or deleting your account only ends renewal or the account respectively. To withdraw from the contract, you must declare this as described above.

Early expiry: The right of withdrawal for a service contract expires when we have fully performed the service and began performance only after you gave express consent and simultaneously acknowledged that you would lose your right of withdrawal upon full performance by us.

Google Play purchase: Refunds are handled through Google Play using the payment method used there.

Free trial: The app's 7-day trial is free and does not renew automatically. No payment details are requested. Withdrawal is not needed for it.
