Delete your Lookin account

for the "Lookin – AI Food Tracker" app by Mhd Khair Shikho

Fastest: in the app

  1. Open Lookin and go to Profile.
  2. Tap Privacy & data.
  3. Tap Delete account and account data and confirm with your password.

Your account and associated data are deleted immediately. The app also removes its introduction answers, profile picture, food, water and reminder data from this device. To keep your data, export it first under Profile › Privacy & data.

Without the app

Send an email from the address registered with Lookin to lookinsupport@gmail.com with the subject "Delete Lookin account". We delete your account within one month and confirm by email. To prevent unauthorised account deletion, we process only requests sent from the account's address.

What is deleted

  • Account, email address, name and sign-in data
  • Profile, goals, nutrition style, allergies and intolerances, target weight
  • Diary (meals and quantities), weight and waist measurements
  • Recipe favourites, coach history, trial and Premium status
  • Consent records, including immediate subscription commencement, and usage counters

What remains

  • We retain subscription withdrawal declarations (name, email, note, receipt time and IP-address hash) without an account ID as evidence, usually until the three-year limitation period ends. You may still declare withdrawal here after deletion.
  • Your email-address hash remains stored for up to three years to prevent repeated free trials. A new account with the same address therefore receives no new trial.
  • RevenueCat purchase data is not deleted automatically. You may request deletion by emailing lookinsupport@gmail.com, subject to retention obligations.
  • Google Play purchase receipts are held by Google and subject to statutory retention periods.
  • Emails you sent us and our withdrawal notifications remain in our mailbox.
  • Server logs, such as IP address and timestamp, are deleted after no more than 7 days.
  • Data on other devices, or after deletion by email, stays on the device until you uninstall the app or clear its data in system settings (in a browser: clear site data).

See section 6 of the Privacy Policy for more information.

Important: cancel your subscription separately

Deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription. Cancel Lookin Premium first in Google Play under Profile › Payments and subscriptions › Subscriptions.
