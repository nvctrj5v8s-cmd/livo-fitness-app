3. What data do we process, why and on what legal basis?

3.1 Account and sign-in

At new registration, we store the confirmed language and text versions of the Terms and the acknowledged privacy and health notices in account metadata, together with the technical registration timestamp. Purpose: documenting the contract and displayed notices (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). This is not blanket consent to optional processing and does not replace AI or health-data consent. These metadata are included in account exports and deleted with the account.

The app follows your device language until you choose German or English in your profile. This preference is stored on this device. When you sign in, we also save the resolved language in your account so account and contract emails arrive in that language. You can change it at any time. Legal basis: providing the selected app language (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).

When you create an account, we store your email address, a hashed password, an account ID, whether your address is confirmed, and technical timestamps such as registration time. Purpose: sign-in and linking your data to your account. Legal basis: performance of a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).

3.2 Profile, targets and diary

When you enter a custom food, its name, entered nutrition values, measurement basis and consumed amount are stored as your diary entry in your account. “Save as my template for later” creates an additional private copy (name, nutrition values, measurement basis, amount and technical template ID) on this device only, isolated by account. It does not enter the public food catalogue and is not automatically sent to a food provider. Purpose: the reuse you request (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). Up to 100 templates remain until you delete them individually, delete your account in the app or remove app/browser storage. Templates are included in the local part of account exports. The demo keeps them in memory only.

Depending on your entries, we store in your account: name, goal, calorie and protein targets, nutrition style, activity level, optional allergies and intolerances, meals and quantities, body measurements (weight and optional waist circumference), a target weight entered in your profile, and recipe favourites. We also store your name in the account at sign-up. Purpose: providing app features and access across devices.

Weight, waist circumference, target weight, allergies and intolerances may be health data. We store them in your account only with your express consent and use them for guideline values, recipe filters and warnings (Articles 9(2)(a) and 6(1)(a) GDPR). The app requests consent with a separate, unchecked box. Without consent, allergies, target weight and introduction answers stay on your device only, and weight entries are unavailable. You may withdraw consent at any time under Profile › Privacy & data. The app then deletes allergies, target weight, introduction answers and all weight and waist measurements from your account.

3.3 Introduction questions

We store your optional introduction answers (sex, date of birth, height, weight, target weight and pace, motivation, obstacles, calorie-tracking experience, cooking time and an optional health note) on your device. The app uses them to calculate guideline values. With your express consent to health information (see 3.2), we also save these answers in your account so you do not have to enter them again on a new phone, after reinstallation or in another browser (Articles 9(2)(a) and 6(1)(a) GDPR). Without consent, they remain on your device only. We delete the account copy when you withdraw consent, reset your answers in the app or delete your account. A target weight you later edit in your profile is stored in your account (see 3.2). When you use the AI coach, some of these details (motivation, obstacles, experience) may be sent to AI as context (see 3.6). Lookin does not calculate calorie targets for people under 18 or with certain health notes.

This local information may also be health data. You enter it voluntarily and may change or delete it in the app at any time.

3.4 Profile picture

A profile picture stays on your device only and is not uploaded to our servers. It is included in the data export only.

3.5 Camera and barcode scanner

The app requests camera or selected-photo permission for the barcode scanner and AI photo recognition. Barcode scanning sends only the barcode number to our server, which looks it up in Open Food Facts. Your name and account ID are not forwarded. Open Food Facts data is provided under the Open Database Licence.

On Android, the scanner recognises barcodes on the device using Google ML Kit. Google receives technical usage and device data, but no images. The app does not use a microphone or record audio. On iPhone, the scanner recognises barcodes on the device using Apple's Vision framework; no images are sent to us or third parties.

3.6 AI features (Lookin Premium)

The coach also receives fixed, editorially prepared source summaries without user data. Associated source links are stored and exported with the answer in the existing chat history. This collects no new personal details and sends no searches containing your messages to source providers. The overview is available in the coach and on Health information and sources. Opening a source link requests an external website in your browser; that website processes technical connection data, such as your IP address, under its own privacy notice. Lookin does not attach chat, profile or diary data to these links.

The AI consent dated 29 October 2026 includes notices about sources, estimates and medical limits. Earlier positive AI consent is requested again for this version; refusals and withdrawals remain respected. No AI request is transmitted without current express consent. The rest of the app remains available.

AI requests also include the German or English language code so answers and photo descriptions follow your app language. We collect no additional health information for this and use no separate translation service. Previously saved messages keep their original language.

AI photo recognition and the Lookin Coach run through our server (a Supabase Edge Function), which forwards requests to OpenAI (contracting party for EEA customers: OpenAI Ireland Ltd., Ireland; processing also occurs through OpenAI in the USA). The app itself contains no AI key.

  • To check citations, the generated coach answer is sent to OpenAI again with selected source summaries. The answer may include information you provided. This check can also make mistakes.
  • Coach: Your question, goal and daily targets, today's diary totals, nutrition style, allergies and intolerances, activity level, optional introduction information and the last 12 chat messages. Lookin does not add name, email, account ID, date of birth, sex, height, weight, target weight or health notes as profile fields. Information you include in a message or image may still be part of the request. Messages indicating an acute crisis are not sent to OpenAI and are not stored; you receive a fixed response offering help.
  • Photo recognition: The photo is sent for analysis with the same context as the coach, without chat history. We do not store it. OpenAI may retain requests for up to 30 days for abuse detection and does not use them for training. Photo analysis in the coach stores the text answer in chat history. Recognised values are estimates. You check and confirm them before saving.
  • Chat history: Questions and answers are saved per account (but not crisis messages or the accompanying context). At most the last 100 messages from the last 90 days are shown and used. The server deletes older messages automatically (daily deletion run and at the next coach use), or at the latest when you delete your account. You may delete history in the app at any time.

Sending data to OpenAI is based on your express consent (Articles 6(1)(a) and 9(2)(a) GDPR), requested before first use with a separate, unchecked box. It also covers health information you provide in questions or photos. Providing the paid feature itself is based on performance of a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). Without consent, the app sends nothing to OpenAI. You may withdraw consent at any time under Profile › Privacy & data. AI can make mistakes and does not replace professional advice.

We have a data-processing agreement with OpenAI that includes the European Commission's standard contractual clauses for transfers to the USA.

3.7 Trial, Premium subscription and payment

The free trial is an offer of the store (Google Play or the Apple App Store). The store requires a payment method for it, checks whether your Google account or Apple Account has already used the trial, and handles the payment. We receive no payment details and no information about your store account, only the subscription notification through RevenueCat (see below). For the subscription we store whether and until when Premium applies, through which channel (trial, store, developer access) and the time of the last store notification (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). For the provider's own test and developer accounts, we store only a hash of the email address to switch Premium on and off for testing. Payment is made through Google Play or the Apple App Store. The store processes payment data as an independent controller. We do not receive it.

We use RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA) to manage purchases. RevenueCat receives your account ID, purchase data (product, timestamps, store, country) and technical data such as your IP address. It receives no health, profile or diary data. In the Android and iPhone apps, RevenueCat is linked to your account ID at sign-in even if you make no purchase, so later purchases are assigned correctly. Legal basis: performance of a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR), or, without a purchase, our legitimate interest in correct assignment (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). Tax and commercial-law retention obligations for billing data remain unaffected (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR).

We have a data-processing agreement with RevenueCat as part of its Terms of Service, including the European Commission's standard contractual clauses for transfers to the USA.

3.8 Security and abuse prevention

We count requests per account to limit abuse and costs: daily AI requests (coach and photos), up to 100 with a subscription and 50 during the trial, and barcode lookups, up to 12 per minute. Only dates or time windows and counts are stored, not content. Legal basis: legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

3.9 Web version

The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). Visits process technically necessary data such as your IP address. We serve the web version's program components ourselves. Only if the app needs a character missing from bundled fonts may it load a fallback font from Google servers (fonts.gstatic.com), transferring your IP address to Google. In browsers without built-in barcode recognition, the web scanner loads a library through jsDelivr, also transferring your IP address. The Android and iPhone apps are unaffected. Email images (logo and illustrations) are also hosted on GitHub Pages. If your email client displays images, it loads them there and transfers your IP address to GitHub. Our emails use no tracking pixels or click tracking.

This section applies while the web version is publicly accessible. Legal basis: legitimate interest in providing it (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

3.10 Server logs, emails and support

Each connection to our server causes Supabase to process your IP address, timestamp and technical device information in logs. These are deleted after no more than 7 days. Our server functions log error codes only, not message content.

We send address-confirmation, password-reset, purchase-confirmation and withdrawal-confirmation emails through Supabase and Resend (Resend, Inc., USA). Your email address and message content are processed. Purchase confirmations include the plan, price, purchase date, contractual terms and, where applicable, the time of your consent to immediate service commencement. If you contact us, we process your message through our Google (Gmail) mailbox to reply. Legal bases: Article 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR, and Article 6(1)(c) GDPR for purchase and withdrawal confirmations (statutory confirmation obligations).

If you report a coach answer, the app sends the report directly to our server. We store the chosen reason, the reported answer (no more than 2,000 characters), the language, the time and your account ID, and receive an email notification in our mailbox (see above). The report is not sent to OpenAI. We use it to review errors and unsafe answers and to improve the coach (legitimate interest in a safe service, Article 6(1)(f) GDPR; where the answer contains health information, based on your consent to AI processing, Article 9(2)(a) GDPR). We delete reports after no more than 12 months, or earlier with your account.

3.11 Consent records

When you give or withdraw consent, we store its type, your decision, the timestamp and the displayed text version in your account and as a copy on your device. Account records cannot be changed; withdrawal is a new record. This allows us to demonstrate consent (Articles 7(1) and 6(1)(c) GDPR). The same applies, together with the selected plan, to your express request that a purchased subscription begins immediately (Article 6(1)(b) and (c) GDPR).

3.12 Withdrawal from a subscription

When you withdraw through the app or website, we store your name, email, optional note (such as order number), account ID if signed in, receipt timestamp and whether confirmation emails were sent. We send you an acknowledgement and ourselves a notification with these details (including your account email if signed in) to our mailbox (see 3.10). This processes and documents withdrawal (Article 6(1)(b) and (c) GDPR). To prevent abuse (at most 5 website withdrawals per connection per day), we also store a hash of your IP address created with a secret key, not the IP address itself (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

6. How long do we store data?

  • Account data, profile, diary and favourites: until you delete your account.
  • An automatic deletion run on our server enforces the following periods every night.
  • Coach history: up to 100 messages from the last 90 days; older messages are deleted automatically every day. You may delete it at any time.
  • Reports of coach answers: no more than 12 months.
  • Trial and subscription status: until account deletion. Store billing data and our billing data are subject to statutory retention periods.
  • AI and barcode request counters: until account deletion. They contain no content.
  • Server logs: no more than 7 days.
  • Consent records, including immediate subscription commencement: until account deletion.
  • IP-address hash for website withdrawals: no more than 24 hours.
  • Subscription withdrawal declarations: until the statutory limitation period expires (usually three years from the end of the year received), including after account deletion, then without the account ID.
  • Deleting your account does not automatically delete RevenueCat purchase data. You may request deletion at the email address above, subject to retention obligations.
  • Emails in our mailbox (support and withdrawal notifications): no longer than three years after the matter is closed (limitation period); withdrawal notifications are kept no longer than withdrawal declarations.
  • Account deletion removes personal database entries. Our current Supabase plan has no automatic backups that we can access. If we use daily backups in future, deleted data is overwritten there after no more than 7 days. If we create manual backups, we keep them for no more than 30 days, then overwrite them.
  • Resend stores sent emails and delivery logs under its own retention rules; it does not publish a fixed period. Resend backups are overwritten after 7 days. You may request deletion through us.
  • Deleting your account does not cancel an active store subscription. You must cancel it in the store.

7. Data on your device

The app stores technically necessary information on your device (Android and iPhone: app storage; web: local browser storage), such as your session, introduction answers, copies of consent decisions, the confirmation that you are at least 18 years old (a "No" answer is not stored), reminder preferences, profile picture, saved and recently used foods, your water intake of the last seven days and notice markers (for example for the paywall and for resetting the password). Deleting your account in the app removes its introduction answers, profile picture, food, water and reminder data from the device. This is strictly necessary to operate the app (section 25(2)(2) TDDDG). There are no tracking or advertising cookies. Android system backup of this app's data to your Google account is disabled. On iPhone, this data can be part of your iCloud or device backup if you have turned it on; Apple manages that backup for you.

8. Data security

Transfers are encrypted (HTTPS). Access to database tables containing personal data is restricted so signed-in users can read and change only their own data. Secret keys are kept exclusively on the server.
